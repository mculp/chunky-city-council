# Playing together

Step 1 of the bible's "Playing together" (CHUNKY-151): everyone in town sees everyone else, by name and by look, walking, running, driving and swinging, anywhere they are, and on the map. Up to 25 at once.

## Trying it on this machine

```sh
npm run net:server
python3 -m http.server
```

Open http://localhost:8000/?net in two windows and press Play in each. From a phone on the same network it's `http://<this machine's address>:8000/?net`, since the town is looked for on the same host as the page. `?net=ws://…` points at a town somewhere else. On the game's own site, which is https, `?net` alone is the town everyone shares on Cloudflare, `TOWN_URL` in `src/net.js`, since a page on https can't open a plain ws:// socket.

The owner's link is `#hero=<secret>` on the end of the address, for a town started with `CHUNKY_HERO` set to the secret's sha256 in hex:

```sh
CHUNKY_HERO=$(printf '%s' "$SECRET" | shasum -a 256 | cut -d' ' -f1) npm run net:server
```

The phone keeps the secret once the link's been opened, for the town it came with and no other, and it comes off the address straight away so it isn't passed on.

The town as it's hosted for everyone runs on this machine in Cloudflare's own runtime too, without signing in to anything:

```sh
npx wrangler@4.154.0 dev --config server/wrangler.jsonc --port 8788 --var CHUNKY_HERO:<the sha256>
```

and it's `?net=ws://localhost:8788/town`. `npm run town:host` checks it there.

## What happens

- **The title card.** It's one thing, Play, with how many are in town right now under it ("Nobody in town right now" when there's nobody), and nothing of the story: no Start, Skip to town, Forget everyone or chapter. Behind it is the gas station at night with its pumps lit, from across US 80, where everyone comes to, drifting as the bridge's camera does on chunky.ms. The first time, Play asks for a name and a look. After that it takes them straight in as who they were, and Change your look under it opens the panel they joined with, since there's no pause screen to change it from in town yet.
- **Joining.** A name of up to 16 letters, checked for PG-13 on the phone and again by the town, and one of the town's eight crowd looks, picked or rolled from the name.
- **Starting.** Everyone starts out front of the gas station on US 80, by the pumps where Coop can see them, facing him and each a little apart from the others (CHUNKY-169). That's where they start the first time and every time they come back. Where they're put is checked first: on dry ground, inside nothing, near enough for Coop to turn to them, and with nothing between them and him. Nobody watches the opening, the owner included. Tadpole comes with CHUNKY-112.
- **No story.** A town that's shared has no chapters, the hero's included, so the objective box says nothing to anyone until the side quests (CHUNKY-163). Everyone's beans are a count, "Beans 5", and twelve of them is just twelve, with no end card. "Swim to the bank." still shows for anyone in the water in town.
- **Down, and back up.** A try that ends is the story's own fever, of whatever got them, and the others see them lying where they went down. Then they come to by the pumps, heart first, with what they had.
- **Coming back.** The town keeps their name, look, where they were, their beans, pockets and gas, and what the cast remember of them and how they feel about them, by the hash of a key their phone makes and keeps for that town. They come back out front of the gas station with what they had, and not where they were standing. It keeps the thousand who've been here last, so someone who's been away while a thousand others came comes back as someone new to it, with nothing, and the cast don't remember them.
- **The cast.** Coop, Rocky and Miss Bobby say something new to whoever comes by, every time the model can: the hero as the story has him, and anyone else as someone from Chunky who quit too and un-quit, by the name they gave and as "they". When it can't, it's a line written for online, each once to each player, and once those are all said they look at whoever it is and nod. How they feel about each player is kept and told them, moved the way the story's meters move, so Coop warms to someone and cools on someone else. Nothing from the chapters is said to anyone, and the 988 Lifeline is the same as anywhere. `data/talk.md` has the rest.
- **The owner.** The hero is the owner's alone, and only one phone at a time can be him: a second one takes over, and the first is told. He starts out front of the gas station like everyone else, with no chapters, which stay at chunky.ms.
- **Everyone else.** Everyone else is a townsperson in their look, on their own screen too, standing all the way up and not glowing. Their names float over them within 40 m, and they're blue dots on both maps, with their names in blue on the big one, where the cast are cyan. The hero's dot is the orange of his own arrow. On the big map, names take turns for room: the hero's, then everyone else playing, then the cast, then places and streets. A name further down goes to the other side of its dot, or is left out if there's no room there either, and every dot shows. WAVE is a button on a phone, where SHAMBLE is in a game on your own, and V on a keyboard.
- **Vehicles.** A vehicle is known by its place in the game's list, so it's the same one on every phone. Someone driving it is seen in it, and it's left exactly where they get out, for everyone. That's so even jumping out of one that's going: it stops where they jumped, and only they go rolling. Nobody can get in one someone else is in: its handle rattles, and he can see who's in it.
- **Nobody hurts anybody.** A swing goes through people. A vehicle someone else is driving nudges you out of its way on your own phone, without harm.
- **Wearing your own rektguy** (CHUNKY-170). Connect a wallet is under Play on the title card, once the town's said it can pair one. The phone asks to pair and gets a code and four letters, with a button for each of MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet and Phantom, which opens the game's small page, `wallet.html`, in that app's own browser with the code. Any other wallet has Copy the link. A browser with a wallet of its own, as on a laptop, has a button for it as well, which opens the page in a tab beside the game. The page shows the same four letters and asks the wallet for its address and to sign plain words: that it's to wear their rektguy in Chunky, the four letters, the date, and that it costs nothing and moves nothing. The town checks the signature and reads from the chain which rektguys the wallet holds. The phone that asked, which has been asking the town every second and a half whether it's done, is told them by handle, and nothing else, and they're over the town's looks, by their pictures. One is worn straight away, and with several they choose, and change it there whenever they like. A wallet with none, one that never signs and one that backs out all leave them with the town's looks, with nothing said. Everyone's told the handle of what someone's wearing, and has its picture by it. Whoever's wearing rektguy 1386, the owner's own, is the hero, in the hero's own look.

## What isn't shared yet

- The crowd in the street stays out until step 3, and so do drivers, kids on four-wheelers and trains. People at home and the cast are each phone's own.
- Wrecks stay on each phone until step 2.
- How much is wrecked or burnt, what the crowd remember and the church's pantry are all still kept per browser, as they are on your own.

## Where it is

- `src/net.js`: what goes over the wire, and how someone's steps are smoothed. Poses are 18 bytes, twenty times a second each way, and everything else goes as JSON words. Each phone sends about 400 bytes a second, and gets about 400 a second for each other person in town, so 25 in town is under 10 KB a second down.
- `src/names.js`: the PG-13 check.
- `server/town.js`, `server/host.js` and `server/index.js`: the town, the town behind any socket, and a Node server for it on this machine.
- `server/worker.js`: the town hosted for everyone, as the Durable Object `Town`, beside the characters' words. `/town/here` says how many are in town right now, for the title card, to the same pages `/town` lets in. The town on this machine says it at `/here`, to any page, as any page can join it.
- `src/together.js`: the phone's side. It covers the title card's Play and how many are in town, the panel, where everyone starts, everyone's bodies (the crowd's own, in six draw calls however many there are), the hero as others see him, names over heads, map dots, vehicles and WAVE, and Connect a wallet, with their own rektguys among the looks.
- `src/wallet.js`: the phone's side of pairing with a wallet, and the wallet's page's, which is `wallet.html`, beside `index.html` and put up with it.
- `src/talk.js` and `data/talk.json`: what the cast say to whoever's come by, and the lines written for online.
- `server/wallet.js`: pairing a phone with a wallet, what's kept of the wallet by whose phone it is, and the wallet's side of the town as the phone and the wallet's page ask it, at `/wallet/` and `/rektguy/<handle>`, for Cloudflare and this machine alike. `server/chain.js`: what's read from the chain, which is whose signature it is, what a wallet holds and what each rektguy looks like. `tools/chain-stand-in.mjs` stands in for the chain in the tests and the checks.
- `index.html`: a few lines hang on `net`, which is null in a game on its own, and the title card over the gas station on the page being the town that's shared, so the opening is the same to the byte (`npm run frames`).
- `npm run net`: the check, `npm run wallet` wearing your own rektguy's, and `npm run town:host` the hosted town's.

## Calls made along the way

- **Where the town is.** One town for everyone, the Durable Object `Town` in `server/worker.js` on Cloudflare, beside the talk server. It's made in the eastern US the first time anyone comes, which is the nearest to Mississippi. It beats twenty times a second while anyone's in it, is kept every 5 s and once the last one goes, and then rests. Its address is `TOWN_URL` in `src/net.js`.
- **Keeping the town.** It's written without holding up the snapshots (`allowUnconfirmed`), so a crash loses at most the last 5 s of where people were. Everyone it keeps is one value, which Cloudflare holds to 2 MB, and once that's full nothing more could be kept at all. A key costs nothing to make, so a script could get there, and a changed page could leave vehicles at all 65,536 places a pose can name. So the town keeps the thousand who've been here last, and only the first 4,096 places in the list, which is far more than there are vehicles. Each person is about 180 bytes as Cloudflare keeps them, and 1.1 KB at most whatever their phone says they carry, which comes to under 1.4 MB with every place full, as `server/town.test.js` measures. Whoever's been away longest is let go of first, never anyone who's here, and what the cast remember of them is deleted with them. For more than a thousand, each person would want an entry of their own, as what the cast remember has.
- **Two things Cloudflare's sockets do differently.** Since compatibility date 2026-03-17, a socket taken with `accept()` hands over bytes as a `Blob`, which the town would drop. So the town takes them with `acceptWebSocket`, whose `webSocketMessage` always gets an `ArrayBuffer`. And wrangler dev doesn't answer a phone's close, whatever the compatibility date says, so the town answers it itself. A phone waits for that before it's closed.
- **What it costs.** Cloudflare bills 20 incoming WebSocket messages as one request. Twenty-five people for two hours is about 180,000, which is past the free plan's 100,000 a day, so playing for real is the $5 a month plan. The free plan does for building and a few testers. Whether the free plan's 100,000 counts messages 20 to one too, Cloudflare doesn't say.
- **The town behind the title card.** On a page that's the town everyone shares, the town's on behind the title card from the first frame, as it is when it's shared: the opening's things put away and no trains. Its comings and goings wait until `src/together.js` is loaded, which is a moment, since it's `net` that keeps the drivers off the roads, and a driver who'd started before then would stop dead in the road once it was on.
- **How many are in town.** The title card asks the town's `/here`, beside its own address, when it comes up and every 10 s while it's up and the tab's showing. Nothing's said under Play if there's nobody to ask.
- **A town says it can pair a wallet.** `/here` says `wallets: true` for a town that can, and Connect a wallet is only offered, and what's theirs only asked, once it has. So the game's files can go up before the town that pairs does, and the button comes by itself when it's there. Before then a town on Cloudflare answers `/wallet/` with a 404 that no page may read, which would be an error in every console.
- **Staying connected.** A phone that says nothing for 5 s is let go. A dropped line tries again by itself, and a newer phone with the same key, or the hero's link, takes over.
- **Different versions of the game.** Each phone sends a fingerprint of the parked vehicles. A town only mixes games whose vehicles are the same, since that's what the list's places mean.
- **Parking exactly.** Getting out sends one last pose from the vehicle at once, so the town leaves it exactly where they did. Another phone ignores that rider's older poses from in it, even though smoothing puts those a little way back down the road.
- **Getting in and out isn't smoothed.** Everyone's shown a tenth of a second behind, and what they're in and how they are go with where they're shown, from the same pose. Getting in or out is a jump: they're just there. Before, what they were in came from the newest pose, so a pickup jumped to where its driver had been standing on everyone else's phone for a tenth of a second.
- **Who a phone is, per town.** A phone's key, which is its place in a town, and the hero's link are each kept for the town they came with, by the `?net` on the address, and never go to another. Otherwise a link to someone else's town would hand over both. The name and look go anywhere.
- **Sitting in anything.** The crowd only drives cars, pickups and four-wheelers, so `SEATED` in `index.html` only has those. In anything else, someone sits as the hero does, going by the car: as much lower as his seat is, and as much smaller as he is there. The pickup's numbers come out of the car's the same way, and it's what keeps a head under a Camaro's roof.
- **Everyone else's colour on the map.** Blue, `#5b8cff`, measured against everything else drawn there in CIEDE2000, for usual eyes and for each kind of colour blindness. For every eye it's at least 15 from the cast's cyan, the hero's orange, the red of where to go and the cream of the roads, cars and street names, and it's 6.3 to 1 against the page. White was the first thought, but it's 11 from the cream, so a player's name would read as a street's.
- **The big map keeps everyone moving.** The game stops while it's open, as it always has, so you stand still while you look. Everyone else playing doesn't: the map's drawn again five times a second while anyone else is in town, with each of them where they are now, so nobody's sent walking to where someone was. The phone carries on talking to the town all the while, on a loop of its own, so nobody's let go for reading the map. When the crowd comes to the shared town, with CHUNKY-153, opening the map can't stop the world for anybody, and that's settled then.
- **The hero as others see him.** He's built from `HERO_BUILD`, beside the hero in `index.html`, with a face of his own, so a change to how he's built shows on everyone else's phones too.
- **No hammer for townsfolk.** A swing is the arm going over and down.
- **Who the cast are talking to.** Everything the model's told about whoever's come by that changes for someone who isn't the hero is written once, with `{They}`, `{them}` and the like, and `src/talk.js` puts in his or theirs. So there's one place for each line, and the hero's ask is the same to the letter as it was, which a test checks by its hash. The player's name only goes in as a short name in quotes, with anything but letters, figures, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes taken out, so a name can't tell the model anything. Which written line they say, and which when nothing new comes back in time, are each one function, `writtenOpener` and `writtenFallback`.
- **What the cast remember, kept apart.** Everyone the town keeps is one value, which Cloudflare holds to 2 MB, and what the cast remember of one person can come to tens of KB. So each player's is an entry of its own, `mind:<key hash>`, read when they say hello and written with the town every 5 s, 128 to a put, with any that don't go tried again next time. Anyone the town lets go of has theirs deleted the next time it's kept. If it can't be read, they're let go to try again, rather than come in as a stranger and have it written over. A phone sends each change as it happens, a line said, a note kept, a visit over or how they feel as it is now, which keeps everything it says under the 2 KB a phone may say at once. How they feel goes as it is, not as how far it moved, and the town holds it to the story's ranges. On this machine it's in the same file as the town, beside what the town keeps itself.
- **Pairing with the wallet's own browser.** A phone's browser has no wallet, and the CD chose one trip to the wallet app over loading a wallet library into the game or an account for the owner. The words are in the shape EIP-4361 gives them, so a wallet shows which site's asking, and they're a signature only: nothing is ever a transaction, and nobody's ever asked for a seed phrase. Only the game's own pages are answered, and the words name the page that asked.
- **Nobody's told a wallet's address.** Only the town has it, and only the wallet's own page sees it, in the words it signs. Phones are told a rektguy by a handle made at random once and kept, the same for everyone, since its number would lead anyone to the wallet. Nobody's told how many a wallet holds but the phone that asked.
- **The wallet apps' own links.** Each button is the link that app's own developer docs give for opening a page in its browser. The code's in the page's query rather than after a #, since a wallet app's link can lose what's after one, and the page takes it off its address as soon as it's read, keeping it for that tab in case the wallet's browser loads the page again. It names no page it came from, so the code isn't passed on.
- **Checked before it's signed.** The wallet's page only asks for a signature on words that are exactly what they should be, whoever sent them: this page asking, for the wallet in front of it, with the four letters on the phone, on Ethereum, and saying it costs nothing and moves nothing. So nothing answering in the town's place can have a wallet sign anything else through the game's page.
- **Kept where it outlives a pause.** The phone's page is put away while the player's in their wallet app, and a Durable Object nobody's asked anything for a minute or so can be put away too. So a pairing is kept in the town's storage, not its memory, for its five minutes, one to a phone and a thousand at once at most. What a wallet holds is kept by the hash of the phone's key, read again in the background once it's been an hour, so a rektguy that's been sold comes off, and forgotten when the town lets that phone go.
- **In the town, not the Worker.** On the free plan a Worker gets ten milliseconds of CPU for each ask and a Durable Object gets half a minute, and checking a wallet means reading thousands of answers from the chain. So the Worker only passes `/wallet/` and `/rektguy/` to the town, after holding pairing, the words and a signature to thirty a minute from one address, the same limit the talk server uses. viem comes to 155 KiB of the Worker, gzipped.
- **What a wallet holds.** rektguy's contract can't list what an address holds, so it's asked how many first, which is all there is to it for a wallet with none, and then whose each one is, a thousand to an ask through Multicall3, until as many as it holds have been found. A rektguy in another wallet counts for one it's delegated to by delegate.xyz, either registry, for all of the wallet's, rektguy's or the one, while that wallet still has it. A delegation only for something in particular doesn't count. An ordinary wallet's signature is checked without asking the chain, and a smart wallet's by EIP-1271, or EIP-6492 if it's not on the chain yet. The chain's asked through Ethereum's public nodes, unless the owner puts their own in the secret `ETH_RPC_URL`.
- **Pictures.** Found from what tokenURI says, with anything on IPFS fetched through one gateway, `IPFS_GATEWAY`, ipfs.io's unless it's said, since the gateway an old address names can be gone, and rektguy's first one is. Only https, IPFS, Arweave and what's written into the address itself are fetched from, and only a PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or SVG by its own first bytes, of 8 MB at most. It's served with nothing in it able to run, the town keeps 24 MB of them to hand, and whoever has one keeps it a day.
- **The nod.** The cast's heads are part of their hoods, so a nod turns the top half of them a little further over about the hips, the way they already stand over, and back up, with their arms hung from wherever that puts their shoulders. Their feet stay where they are, and they're exactly as they were once it's over.
