# Chunky 2026

A phone-first survival game set in post-apocalyptic Chunky, Mississippi. The backlog is the "chunky survival" project in Linear (team CHUNKY).

Read `docs/art-direction.md` before changing anything visible. `reference/stuckeys-bridge.html` is reference material, not part of the game. Don't edit it.

The owner sends lore for the story. It goes in `docs/lore.md` in their words as soon as it's sent, built or not. Nothing gets added to it.

## Making decisions

- Technical questions: decide, and record the assumption in the commit.

## Stack

- A static site with no bundler and no build step. All the code is in `index.html` today. New code can split into ES modules under `src/`.
- three.js is pinned to 0.160.0 from jsDelivr by the importmap in `index.html`.

## Data

- Game data is JSON under `data/`, such as `data/roads.json`, with its schema documented alongside it.
- Roads start from Census TIGER/Line data and buildings from FEMA and ORNL's USA Structures, which is CC BY 4.0, so its credit stays with it. Street View and the satellite, Google Earth's included, are what they're checked and corrected against, and none of Google's imagery is committed. What a building looks like, and where its footprint's wrong, is in `tools/structure_notes.json`, in words and without street addresses.
- The smaller roads and a drive to every building are in `data/lanes.json`, documented in `data/lanes.md`. To everything that asks what road is here they're roads, but `MAP.roads` stays the graph.
- The big farm south of the Chunky River Bridge, where the woods stop and its fences, is in `data/farm.json`, documented in `data/farm.md`.
- Dialogue and the relationship meters are in `data/cast.json`, documented in `data/cast.md`.
- When Coop, Rocky and Miss Bobby would only say a line they've said before, they can say something new in their own words, from a cheap model through the talk server, with the written line whenever there's nothing new in time. That, what they remember of him and the 988 Lifeline are in `src/talk.js` and `data/talk.json`, documented in `data/talk.md`. With `?test` nothing's asked until `__game.talk.on(url)`.
- What's in the pantry at the church on Church St, and how it fills back up a day at a time, is in `src/pantry.js`, also documented in `data/cast.md`. Its days are the church signs' Mississippi days.
- What the people holding out come up with for him to do is in `src/quests.js` and `data/quests.json`, documented in `data/quests.md`, and where they can send him is `data/places.json`, documented in `data/places.md`. Once a day Jev decides what and where through the talk server's `/decide`, and everything that's said is written. The first is Coop's fishing hole, and the fishing is `src/fishing.js`. It's only in the story for now: in a town that's shared he keeps the counter. With `?test` nothing's asked until `__game.talk.on(url)`, `__game.coop.decide(fn)` stands in for Jev, and `__game.churchSigns.clock(ms)` sets the clock in Mississippi.
- Toni, who catches frogs at the burned house on Gallatin St and shows them to whoever comes by, is in `src/toni.js`, and what she says is in `data/toni.json`, documented in `data/toni.md`.
- Everyone in town has a name, a personality and a memory of what he's done: `data/folks.json`, `src/agents.js`, documented in `data/folks.md`. None of it comes from `rnd()` or `crowdRand`, and half of everybody's an Alexander or a Clark and an eighth are Gressetts, which is the owner's. Most of the crowd have gone quiet and leave him be until he starts something, and the few who've burnt down stop, turn to him and flare for a second before they come, with nothing on the screen saying why, no more than three at once and giving up after eight seconds without him in sight, and noise rouses them and nothing draws them, which is in `src/horde.js` and the same document. Only the ones nearby are out: the people at home are put back once he's 300 m off, and come out again just as they were. Most houses have someone at home, there's someone driving on the real roads and there are kids on four-wheelers on the lanes, which is in `src/traffic.js` and the same document. With `?test` all of those stay away until `fillTown()`, so that every test that isn't about them is left alone.
- The trains run on TIGER's railroad, one at a time and near wherever he is, with crossings that shut and three trestles he can walk: `src/trains.js`, documented in `data/railroad.md`. The words on the marker by the west trestle are in `data/railroad.json`. Nothing says whose railroad it is. With `?test` the train stays away until `trains.go()`.
- Nobody dies. The train throws the crowd clear of the tracks and they lie in the ditch longer than anything else keeps anyone down, then get up sore and go home. The river's cottonmouths have whoever the train throws in, and the current takes them, and they come up downstream on the bank, soaked, with a snake on a sleeve, and walk home: the same people, with what they remember, and nobody new walking in to take their place. Where they come up is `src/river.js`, and the rest is in `src/horde.js` and `data/folks.md`.
- How high the land stands is in `data/hills.json`, documented in `data/hills.md`. The world's flat, and it's only what's drawn that goes up and down. A new `ShaderMaterial` with a vertex shader of its own has to `#include <hill_pars>` and use `hillOver`, or define `FLAT_WORLD`, and anything long goes through `HILLS.follow`.
- What stops anyone, and the floors raised off the ground, are in `data/solids.json` for the town everyone shares, which has no page to ask, documented in `data/solids.md` and loaded by `src/solids.js`. Every height in it is over the town's flat ground. `npm run solids -- --write` writes it from the game's own, and has to be run again after anything that stops anyone has changed. The water in that town, and the river's banks down to its bed, are `src/wet.js`, the game's own ground without its rise and fall, which is in `data/water.md`. Who's at home where in that town, every vehicle where it's parked and the lights that draw the crowd at night are in `data/crowd.json`, documented in `data/crowd.md`, which `npm run crowd -- --write` writes from the game's own and has to be run again after any of them has changed. The world the town's people walk in is `server/world.js`, which has all of it, and `server/crowd.js` runs them in it, which is in the same document.
- What the church signs say is in `data/signs.json`, documented in `data/signs.md`, and all of it can be read at `#signs`.
- What one stone in the cemetery says is in `data/stone.json`, documented in `data/stone.md`. It's the owner's to fill in: nobody else types those words. It's on the slab they picked. Their own song, `audio/music/stone.mp3`, which they added, plays when he pulls up at the cemetery and when the stone's read, as music and not from the stone, once a visit, and `src/song.js` has when. The checks play a stand-in for it, never theirs.
- Fast travel's first part is ten rings of pale mushrooms, on the map from the start: standing in one he eats one, taps another on the map and comes to sitting up in it. About where they are is `data/rings.json`, documented in `data/rings.md`, and where each settles and when each part of the trip happens is `src/rings.js`. None is in anybody's yard.
- What every sound and part of the score is, and how loud, is in `data/sound-new.json`, documented in `data/sound.md`. That's what the game plays: music and sounds made with ElevenLabs, which the owner chose. A file in `audio/` and one line there take a sound's place, without touching the code. The sounds worked out from code stay too, and nothing writes over them: `data/sound.json` is their sheet, `#sounds` is their board and `#old` plays the game with them.
- Scripts that generate it go in `tools/`. Commit the generated JSON. Raw downloads such as TIGER zips go in `data/raw/`, which is gitignored.
- Python tools run from a `.venv/` at the repo root, also gitignored, with dependencies pinned in `tools/requirements.txt`. Don't install anything globally.

## Coordinates

- 1 unit = 1 m. +X is east, +Y is up and -Z is north.
- Map data has its origin at town center, 32.32583, -88.92806, and uses a local equirectangular projection with R = 6,371,000 m: `x = R * dLon * cos(lat0)`, `z = -R * dLat`, in radians.
- The game's world is that map moved over so that its origin is Stuckey's Bridge, where Stuckey Bridge Rd really crosses the river, so the opening scene never moves. Town center is about 6.8 km west and 7.8 km north of it. The map is true scale. Only the road and river right at the bridge are bent, for the opening. `src/map.js` converts everything, and `data/roads.md` has the formulas. Place new things with `atTrue(x, z)`, in true metres from town center.
- What breaks is in `src/damage.js`, and how it looks and what breaks it is in `index.html` under "what burns and what goes up". A piece is known by its place in its building, so damage survives a cell being dropped and written again. Stuckey's Bridge doesn't break.
- In the town everyone shares, what's broken is the same for everyone, and heals a day after it broke once nobody's near. How it's said is `src/wrecks.js`, the town keeps it in `server/town.js`, and `src/together.js` sends it and takes it, laid quietly where it lies unless it's in front of him. Nothing at the places `src/aside.js` sets aside breaks there at all. `docs/multiplayer.md` has the rest.
- Keep the opening as it is. The first scene's terrain, trees and random sequence are deliberately untouched inside its square, which is why `firstSceneH` exists and why new code doesn't call `rnd()`. Use `hash()` for anything new that needs to be random and repeatable.

## Running it

```sh
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.

`npm run net:server` runs the town everyone shares on this machine, at ws://localhost:8787, and keeps it in `data/raw/town.json`. What goes over the wire is in `src/net.js`, the town itself is `server/town.js`, and `server/host.js` is the town behind any socket, which is how the same town goes on Cloudflare. `npm run net:server -- --talk` also answers the characters' words at http://localhost:8787/talk, by the talk server's own rules but with one canned line and nothing spent, for pages on this machine, and `startServer({ talk: { line, quiet } })` does the same in a test. It answers what they come up with at /decide too, with the first shape, the first place and worth it, or what `startServer({ talk: { decide } })` says (`data/talk.md`). It pairs phones with wallets too, for wearing their own rektguy, at /wallet/ for pages on this machine, asking Ethereum's public nodes or `ETH_RPC_URL`'s, and Alchemy's NFT API with `ALCHEMY_KEY` for what a wallet holds and its pictures, and a test hands `startServer({ chain })` one on `tools/chain-stand-in.mjs`, which stands in for the chain so nothing real is asked. It's `server/wallet.js` and `server/chain.js`, and `docs/multiplayer.md` says how it fits.

`index.html?net` is the town everyone shares, as online.chunky.ms is, for that town, and `?net=ws://…` for one somewhere else. That town's on Cloudflare, and on chunky.ms `?net` sends a phone on to online.chunky.ms before anything else loads, with what it keeps for the town, so nobody comes in as someone new. That's the first thing in `index.html`. There's nothing of the story in it: its title card is Play, with how many are in town right now under it, over the gas station from across US 80, and Play asks who they are the first time and takes them straight in after that, with Connect a wallet under it, for wearing their own rektguy, and Change your look for anyone who's been before. Without it there's none of that and nothing of playing together is loaded, and `npm run frames` checks that the opening's the same to the byte. The phone's side of it is `src/together.js`, and `docs/multiplayer.md` says how it fits together. The owner is the hero on a link of their own: the address with `#hero=` and a secret, for a town started with `CHUNKY_HERO` set to that secret's sha256 in hex. Their phone keeps it for that town and no other, as every phone does the key it plays as there, and it comes off the address as soon as it's read.

`npm run deploy` puts main up at https://chunky.ms, the game's own files and nothing else: `index.html` and the wallet's page `wallet.html`, `src/`, `data/` and `audio/`, without tests, Markdown or `data/raw/`. A favicon.ico made from the page's own icon goes with them, and a version.txt that's the commit, which it waits to see on chunky.ms three times running. It takes `--ref` for another commit and `--dry` to list the files.
